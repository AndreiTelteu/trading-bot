## Ingestion and replay interpretation

`internal/services/exchange.go` (`FetchOHLCVPage` and `parseOHLCV`) fetches bounded public klines and parses field 5 as volume without changing zero to another value. `cmd/marketdata/main.go` copies that volume into a point-in-time bar, marks the source bar `valid`, and computes quote volume as close times base volume. This adapter does **not** preserve Binance's trade count or original quote volume, so the external raw evidence above matters. `internal/pointintime/ingest.go` accepts a contiguous zero-volume bar, stores its volume and provenance, and reports missing intervals as unresolved instead of synthesizing bars. `internal/pointintime/manifest.go` checks timestamp gaps and quality status but does not treat a source-reported zero-volume bar as a gap. `internal/pointintime/validated_repository.go` selects the stored volume and passes it into replay. These paths explain how a valid, present zero-volume source bar can reach a Stage 07 fold; no synthetic gap-fill path was found in this ingestion chain.

The Stage 05 `backtest-execution-v2` guard rejects an **intended fill** on this exact bar as `execution_bar_liquidity_unavailable`. The earlier 41-fill fold's one zero-volume AVAX fill is therefore consistent with source data but is not valid v2 fill evidence.
